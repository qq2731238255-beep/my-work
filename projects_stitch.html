OmniHub Smart Control Center

Smart Home Product Design Proposal: OmniHub Smart Control Center

First, let’s look at its appearance.

Appearance

Crafted from eco-friendly materials, the OmniHub features an aerospace-grade aluminum alloy frame combined with ABS engineering plastic, making it both lightweight and durable. Its matte-finish coating is anti-fingerprint and scratch-resistant, creating a premium tactile experience. The size is compact and space-efficient: 200mm × 150mm × 80mm, suitable for tabletops, entryways, or living room corners without disrupting home aesthetics. It comes in two minimalist color options: Pure White and Deep Space Gray.

Next, let’s move on to its function.

Function

This smart control center supports Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, and Zigbee protocols, making it compatible with more than 100 smart devices such as lights, curtains, and security cameras. It offers dual control modes, including a voice assistant with dialect recognition and a 3.5-inch touch screen for instant scene switching. It also supports automated scenarios through custom triggers such as time and sensor data. In addition, the product includes security monitoring with infrared sensors and a 1080p camera for motion detection, real-time mobile alerts, and remote live-view access. It can even track appliance power consumption and generate AI-based energy-saving reports.

Finally, let’s talk about the price.

Price

The Basic Edition is priced at ¥799 (about $110 USD), including device integration, voice and touch control, and basic security monitoring. The Pro Edition is ¥1,299 (about $180 USD), adding an environmental sensor kit, local storage expansion, and 3-year free compatibility updates with priority 24/7 technical support.
